Premiere Landmark Campus

Nizam College, Basheerbagh

One of the premier colleges of Osmania University, Nizam College celebrated its centenary in 1987. The College is one of the oldest and most prestigious institutions of higher education not only in Telangana State but also in India. It was established in 1887 by the amalgamation of the Hyderabad School (Noble School) and Madarsa-I-Aliya. Initially it was affiliated to the University of Madras for 60 years and was made a constituent college of Osmania University...

Our Foundation Ideals

About the University

Osmania University, established in 1917, is the seventh oldest in India, the third oldest in south India and the first to be established in the erstwhile princely State of Hyderabad. Named after its founder, Nawab Osman Ali Khan, VII Nizam, it is a monument to linguistic and modern educational integration.

Our Vision

The Vision of the University is to generate and disseminate knowledge through a harmonious blend of ancient and modern wisdom, and to provide quality education to all.

Our Mission

To achieve excellence in teaching and research. To generate, disseminate and preserve knowledge. To construct a more humane, sustainable, and equitable society.

Our History

Osmania University is named after its founder, Nawab Osman Ali Khan, the seventh Nizam of Hyderabad, whose visionary decree led to the establishment of this historic institution.
